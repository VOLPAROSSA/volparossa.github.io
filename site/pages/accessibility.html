Accessibility is part of making shared technology useful.

Using this site

The site includes a skip link, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings and navigation, responsive layouts and a native mobile menu. Text and links work without JavaScript. Reduced-motion preferences disable optional movement; there is no autoplay video, audio or continuous animation.

Our aim and our limits

WCAG 2.2 Level AA is a development goal, not a claim of certification. Browser and automated checks help find barriers but do not replace a complete audit or independent assistive-technology testing. Third-party websites and upstream interfaces may have their own limitations.

Report a barrier

Open an issue in the website repository ↗ with the page, task, expected result and what happened. Include your browser and assistive technology if useful and you are comfortable sharing them. Text reports are welcome; no diagnosis or personal medical information is needed.

See the project accessibility guide ↗ for broader contribution and reporting guidance.
