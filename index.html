01 / The purpose

More opportunity.
Less dependence.

The goal is everyday software that draws on shared capacity while keeping people in control. One common foundation lets improvements reach several applications.

I.

Reach beyond your device

Ask for help with code, research or a document without making your own computer carry the whole task. We are building shared models and agents for applications to use together; dependable shared AI and protected private tasks remain unfinished.

II.

Keep a say in where data lives

Keep photos, files and messages under your control, with software you can inspect. Our goal is private storage without running your own always-on server. Complete Cloud/Image services and their joined maintenance are still being developed.

III.

Build on tools people know

Firefox, Signal, Thunderbird and other open-source foundations give us useful starting points. A shared forecast can serve two weather clients; a storage repair should help both files and photos. One contribution can improve more than one application.

These are development goals, not coverage, privacy or savings guarantees. Devices need power and reachable links; internet access needs someone's real uplink. Sharing adds transfer and storage costs too. Creators' rights and access permissions still apply.

03 / One route, one relay

Many paths.
A clear privacy boundary.

Each normal internet route is client → exactly one relay → exit → destination. Parallel paths use different relays between the same client and the same selected exit.

The relay knows the client and selected exit, not the internet destination.

The exit knows the destination and incoming relay, not the client's public address.

The destination sees the exit address. A global observer may still correlate low-latency traffic.

The v1 transport design requires genuine MPTCP for TCP, protected single-path QUIC/MASQUE for general UDP, and genuine Multipath QUIC for browser HTTP/3. Ordinary TCP or single-path QUIC does not satisfy a multipath requirement.

Still open

The next shared dependencies

Private-storage maintenance. The ordinary-expiry trial failed. Full Image/Cloud publication, repair, repeated restore and deletion remain unaccepted; the earlier consumer failure and unconfirmed remote cleanup remain separate.

Protected peer computation. The public-model guest trial failed before model execution, with an export inventory pathname rejection. Source checks and public guests do not prove confidential hardware or private-peer execution.

Research boundaries. Transaction trials use fictitious TEST units. Citizen uses fictional identities. Neither establishes real settlement, secure public elections or legal authority.

06 / A shared direction

Modern technology.
Universal wisdom.

Our seven-virtue framework draws inspiration from ethical and philosophical traditions: humility, humanity, gentleness, diligence, generosity, moderation, and respect for consent and personal boundaries.

The intended shared immune system uses mutual review to examine work, challenge unreliable results and support correction across all layers. It is still in development. Decisions need defined authority, explanation and a way to contest them.

Ways to support VOLPAROSSA ↗
Joining is voluntary. Contribution is reciprocal.

Roles start off. Production consumers contribute nonzero relay capacity; nodes with usable independent internet also contribute exit service. Local-only nodes contribute their links without providing an exit. Your own device activity comes first. These responsibilities are distinct from sponsorship.
